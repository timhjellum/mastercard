The Challenge

MasterCard's content and functionality lived across multiple platforms, with no shared taxonomy or navigational structure tying them together. Before any of it could move, someone had to map what existed. I owned the pre-deployment work — content inventory, taxonomy, sitemap, and wireframes — behind a single application built to carry all of it forward.
